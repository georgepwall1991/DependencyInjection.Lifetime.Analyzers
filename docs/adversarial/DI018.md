# DI018 adversarial contract

## Candidate: removed registrations must not remain diagnostics

DI018 reports registrations that MEDI cannot instantiate. A descriptor removed by a later unconditional `RemoveAll`, `Clear`, or `Replace` is not part of the effective service collection and must not produce a stale DI018 diagnostic. A later registration that reactivates the slot must still be analyzed.

### Pre-fix proof

The analyzer iterates `RegistrationCollector.AllRegistrations` directly and ignores `OrderedMutations`. Therefore an abstract implementation registered and then removed still reports DI018 even though the invalid descriptor is absent at runtime.

### Contract

- suppress a non-instantiable registration removed by a definite same-flow mutation;
- preserve the diagnostic for a re-registration after removal;
- model `Clear`, `RemoveAll`, and `Replace` with the collector’s effective-registration replay, including flow/key/body and straight-line conservatism;
- keep conditional or otherwise unproven mutations silent rather than guessing.

## Mutation baseline

Analyzer Stryker baseline (targeted DI018 test suite, Basic mutation level): **92.31%** final score (24 killed, 2 no-coverage, 19 compile-error, 6 block-filter-ignored, and 10,530 mutate-filter-ignored mutants). DI018 has no fixer.

## Mutation post-run

After the effective-registration replay and four regression tests, the same targeted Stryker run also scored **92.31%** (24 killed, 2 no-coverage, 19 compile-error, 6 block-filter-ignored, and 10,530 mutate-filter-ignored mutants), matching the baseline.
